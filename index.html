Our Story

Built by a teacher,
for young learners.

ReaderBears was created by a Filipino teacher who saw firsthand how hard it can be for young children to take their first reading steps — especially in areas where resources are limited.

The goal was simple: build something warm, encouraging, and genuinely effective. Something that feels like a friend sitting beside a child, cheering them on letter by letter.

Today, ReaderBears is used by kids and classrooms across the Philippines — and we're just getting started. 🐾
